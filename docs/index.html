ORHT / LTB · 1998–2025

27 years of tribunal activity, made explorable

Institutions change, sometimes in ways that are difficult to see from year to year. Looking back at what the Landlord and Tenant Board has said about itself, what it measured over the years and how its operations have evolved helps us understand where it has been, where it may be heading and gives us a basis for asking whether that is where we want it to go.

03 / Institutional visibility

What the LTB makes visible though public reports changes over time

Measures are introduced, revised, grouped differently, left out of reports or replaced. A disappearance from annual reporting is evidence of change in public visibility only.

PerformanceThresholds and groupings change

Business-day standards later give way to calendar-day KPIs and a case-lifecycle measure.

MediationDenominators and detail change

The activity remains visible while long-run outcomes become harder to compare consistently.

Service & accessDifferent dimensions appear

Physical locations, filing channels, hearing modes, language and accommodation appear with different levels of detail.
