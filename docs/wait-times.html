Reading the data

What the charts include, and what they do not establish.

Reporting periods and groupings

Tribunals Ontario reports a mixture of fiscal-year and quarterly periods. Not every quarter is available. The charts use province-wide averages. A combined label such as L1/L2 refers to the grouping reported in the source data; it is not an average constructed by this dashboard.

Interpretation

The charts describe reported service times at different process stages. They do not, on their own, explain why a value rose or fell. No missing value is interpolated or replaced with zero.

Source and preparation
